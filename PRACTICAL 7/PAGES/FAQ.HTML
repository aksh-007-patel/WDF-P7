Logging In to Student Hub

Use your university-issued student ID (e.g. 25DCE124) as your username. Your default password is your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format — you will be prompted to change it on first login.

If your account is locked after 5 failed attempts, please wait 30 minutes or contact the IT Help Desk.

Go to Login
🔑

Resetting Your Password

Step 1: Click Forgot Password on the Login page.
Step 2: Enter your registered university email address.
Step 3: Check your inbox for the reset link (valid for 15 min).
Step 4: Set a new password — must be at least 6 characters and include a letter and a number.

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support@charusat.ac.in.

📊

Checking Your Attendance

Your attendance is updated every weekday by 6 PM. You need a minimum of 75 % attendance per subject to be eligible to sit the semester exam.

A red indicator means you are below the threshold. You may raise a grievance from the Dashboard if you believe there is an error.

Open Dashboard
📋

Viewing Exam Results

Results are published on the Dashboard under My Results → Semester. Grades are displayed as SPI (Semester Performance Index) and CPI (Cumulative Performance Index).

To download your official mark sheet, click the Download PDF button. The document is digitally signed and valid for official purposes.
